## Why "60 Minutes or Less" Matters

Water damage is progressive. Within minutes, water spreads to porous materials. Within 24 to 48 hours, **mold growth** begins. That's why our promise is simple: we are on-site in **60 minutes or less** across Central and South Florida.

Unlike general contractors, we specialize in **mitigation first**. We use advanced thermal imaging to map moisture hidden behind cabinets and inside walls, ensuring we dry your home scientifically, not just superficially.

### Handling Your Insurance Claim

Dealing with water damage is stressful enough; fighting with insurance shouldn't be. We use **Xactimate®** software—the industry standard for adjusters—to document every square foot of moisture.

*   **Direct Billing:** We bill the insurance company, not you (in most covered losses).
*   **Documentation:** Comprehensive moisture logs and thermal photos to prove the damage.
*   **Licensed Pros:** We are fully licensed for both Mold Remediation and General Contracting.
