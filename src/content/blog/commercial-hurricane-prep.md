## Is Your Business Ready?

Every hour your business is closed costs money. A solid continuity plan is your best insurance policy.

### 1. Premarket Agreement
Secure a restoration partner *before* the storm. Having a pre-signed agreement means you get priority service when resources are scarce.

### 2. Digital Backups
Ensure all critical data and client records are backed up to the cloud, not just local servers.

### 3. Emergency generator
Have a plan for power. Do you have a generator? Do you have fuel contracts?

### 4. Communication Tree
Who calls who? Establish a clear line of communication for employees.
