## The Workstation Kitchen

The kitchen is no longer just for cooking—it's for working, entertaining, and living.

## 1. The Dual Island
One island for prep, one for seating. This separates the chef from the guests while keeping everyone together.

## 2. Hidden \"Mess\" Pantries
A secondary scullery or walk-in pantry where you can hide the toaster, coffee machine, and dirty dishes.

## 3. Integrated Smart Lighting
Under-cabinet lighting that adjusts color temperature based on the time of day.

## 4. Quartzite vs. Quartz
Why homeowners are moving back to natural stone for its unique veining and durability.
