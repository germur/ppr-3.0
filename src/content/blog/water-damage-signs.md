## 5 Hidden Signs of Water Damage

Living in Florida means dealing with humidity and storms, but water damage isn't always obvious. Here are the 5 silent identifiers:

## 1. Unexplained Spikes in Utility Bills
If your water bill jumps without a change in usage, you might have a slab leak.

## 2. Musty Odors (The Smell of Old Books)
That smell isn't just \"humidity.\" It's often the first sign of microbial growth in your drywall.

## 3. Bubbling Paint
When water seeps behind paint, it separates the layer from the drywall, creating water-filled bubbles.

## 4. Warped Flooring
Laminate or wood floors that start to peak or cup are absorbing moisture from the subfloor.

## 5. Sounds of Running Water
If you hear water running when everything is off, check your meter immediately.
